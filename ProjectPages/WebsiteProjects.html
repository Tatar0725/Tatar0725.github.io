2048

2048 is a game originally made by Gabriele Cirulli in March of 2014. This game took about 3 weeks and 170 Minutes or around 3 hours to complete.

Maze Solver

Maze Solver is made using Depth First Search to create the maze, and Breath First Search to solve the maze. This took around 1 month and around 4 hours and 10 minutes to complete.
